The Coherence Factor

The coherence factor is the master metric that tracks how social systems hold together—or fracture—over time. It combines measurable signals from language, physiology, education, psychology, and policy into a single longitudinal index.

Master Equation

dC/dt = O·G(1-C) - S·C

Inputs

12+ indicators across 4 domains

Timeline

1900s–present, decade-by-decade scoring

Metric blueprint

The coherence factor treats society as an open system. Entropy rises when coherence drops faster than external negentropic inputs can restore it.

  • C (Coherence)Integration of shared meaning and trust
  • S (Entropy)Disorder, fragmentation, institutional decay
  • G (Grace)External negentropic inputs (Logos, truth)
  • O (Observer)Capacity to receive, align, and respond
