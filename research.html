Executive abstract

Theophysics presents a unification of science and spirit, arguing that coherence emerges when systems remain connected to external negentropic input (G/Logos). It frames societal decline as entropy rising faster than coherence can be restored, and it uses the master equation to bridge psychology, language, physiology, and education.
